## The formula

Most software fails by trying to be a platform. I build the opposite: one tool,
one job, done well enough that somebody pays for it. Then I publish what it
earned — including the months it earned nothing.

| | Rule | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **01** | Only ideas with demand that already shows up in search | If nobody is typing it into Google, there is nothing to launch into |
| **02** | No product takes longer than two months | The deadline is the filter. Anything bigger is the wrong scope |
| **03** | Nine out of ten ship to the US and EU | English interface, priced in dollars, distributed where the buyers are |
